Move Along Please

Interact with and move items on map to get the purple ball to the end goal. As you progress through the levels you will learn new mechanics and features, Good luck!

This game is inspired by many puzzle games I played growing up and still enjoy till this day. I wanted to combine some of their features and add a couple of my own. I tried to make each level interesting and new from the previous ones. I tried to be intentional with the mechanics and the progression and I hope that shows...

My goal was to make a puzzle game that starts slow and progressively gets harder as you go. The chill music in the back sets the mood for a chill gameplay and the levels all have infinite lives and tries. Feel free to skip any level you feel is too difficult and come back to it whenever. I just wanted to create a game that makes you think. I hope you enjoy!
